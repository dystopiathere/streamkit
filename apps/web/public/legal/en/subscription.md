## 1. What the plans include

1.1. Alerts, chat, analytics of connected platforms and widgets work on any
plan, including the free one. A plan lifts the limits and adds features.

1.2. **Free plan:** up to four widgets at a time and one connected platform of
the customer's choice. No time limit, no payment required.

1.3. **Multistream:** several connected platforms at once (multistreaming with a
single chat feed and analytics for each platform) and an unlimited number of
widgets at a time.

1.4. **Pro:** everything in Multistream, plus private rooms (creating rooms,
inviting guests by link, video calls with them, and putting their video and
audio into streaming software through the guests widget) and advanced widget
styling (element positions in the frame, fonts, background images and an
extended set of animations).

1.5. The limits of the free plan apply to creating new things: widgets that
already exist are not deleted and keep working. If more platforms are connected
than the plan allows, one of them stays active — the customer chooses which one
in the Analytics section; the others are kept with their settings and do not
work until the plan allows it. Advanced styling settings are kept, but outside
the Pro plan the widget is shown with the basic styling.

## 5. Automatic renewal

5.1. When taking out the plan, the customer separately agrees to the automatic
charging of the next period's price using the saved payment method. The amount
and frequency of the charges are stated in the text of the consent.

5.2. **No later than three days before each charge**, the Operator sends the
customer an email to the account email address: the amount, the date after which
the charge will take place, and a link to turn off auto-renewal. No charge is
made without such an email.

5.3. The charge is made no earlier than one day before the end of the paid period
and no earlier than three days after the email.

5.4. If the charge fails, the Operator retries it no more than once a day, with
no more than three attempts. During this time, access to private rooms is kept
for up to three days after the end of the period. After the last failed attempt,
or after a bank decline that makes a retry impossible, auto-renewal is turned
off.

5.5. Auto-renewal can be turned off at any time with one button in the Plan
section, or by withdrawing consent in the Privacy section. After it is turned
off, nothing is charged, and access remains until the end of the paid period. A
change of period (month or year) applies to the next renewal and requires a new
email about the charge.

6.5. If the service was not provided through the Operator's fault (the plan's

## 7. End of access

7.1. When the paid period ends without renewal, the account moves to the free
plan. Nothing is deleted: widgets and their settings, connected platforms, rooms
and invites are all kept.

7.2. The plan's features stop working: entering a room, giving a guest access
and putting guests on stream are not possible until a new payment; one of the
connected platforms stays active — the customer chooses which one; advanced
widget styling is replaced by the basic one. New widgets beyond the free plan's
limit cannot be created, while the existing ones keep working.

7.3. Deleting the account turns off auto-renewal and deletes the saved payment
method.
