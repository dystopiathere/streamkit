# Room Participation Terms

Version No. 2 of 15.09.2026

This is an English translation provided for convenience. The governing text is
the [Russian version](https://stream-kit.ru/legal/room-guest?lang=ru); if the two
differ, the Russian version prevails.

You have opened an invite link to a streamer's private room on the StreamKit
platform (https://stream-kit.ru). No sign-up is needed. Below is what happens to
your data if you click “Join”.

## 1. What is transmitted

- **The name** you entered before joining. The streamer and other guests see it,
  and it may be shown on stream as a caption under your video.
- **Your camera image and microphone sound** — if you turned them on. The
  streamer and other guests in the room see and hear them.

You can turn off the camera and microphone at any time with the buttons in the
room. The streamer can turn off your microphone.

## 2. Showing you on stream is the streamer's decision and responsibility

The streamer may put your image, voice and name on their stream, that is, show
them to an unlimited audience, and may also record the broadcast by their own
means. **This decision is made by the streamer, who is responsible to you for
it.** Discuss the terms of showing, recording and publishing with the streamer
before joining. The platform only provides the technical means of communication.

## 3. What the platform does NOT do

- **It does not record** video and sound. They are transmitted in real time
  through the platform's media server in Russia and are not stored anywhere.
- **It does not store your name.** It exists only while you take part.
- **It does not use your image or voice to identify you.**

## 4. What is kept

The fact that you agreed to these terms: which link you joined with, the version
of the document, the time, an anonymized fingerprint of your IP address (a hash
from which the address cannot be recovered) and browser details. This is needed
to confirm that consent was given. It is kept for 180 days, after which the
record is deleted.

## 5. How to stop taking part

Close the tab or click “Leave”. The streamer can remove you from the room and
revoke the link.

## 6. Who the operator is and whom to contact

**Name, image and voice.** Their operator is the streamer who invited you. The
platform transmits them on the streamer's behalf and uses them for nothing else.
Send questions and demands about this data, about being shown on stream and about
recording the broadcast to the streamer.
